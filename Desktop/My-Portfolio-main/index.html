Who I Am

About Me

I'm Swarali Warade, a second-year Computer Engineering student at Pune Institute of Computer Technology (PICT), maintaining a 9.48/10 CGPA with a minor in Applied Mathematics.

I love building AI-powered and full-stack products — from a multi-agent platform that simulates startup boardroom discussions, to an AI resume analyzer, to an educational platform for NGO students. My work usually combines React/Next.js on the frontend with Groq, Gemini, and other modern AI APIs on the backend.

I've competed in two hackathons so far: I placed in the top 11 teams in my domain at HackAgentAIx 2026 (hosted by the University of Essex, UK), and my team was a finalist out of 80+ teams at CodeTheCause. I'm currently deepening my full-stack skills through an ongoing course at Finlatics.
